# Code of conduct

Be kind, be specific, assume good faith. Criticise the work, not the person. No harassment, no
discrimination, no publishing of other people's private information. Maintainers may edit,
lock or remove anything that breaks this, and may ask someone to leave the project's spaces.

If something has gone wrong, write to hello@dbmn.io and say what happened. It will be read by a
person, kept confidential, and answered.
